Privacy notice

Last updated 19 August 2026

Asset Signal is pre-launch. This site has one form on it, and this notice describes everything that form does with what you type into it.

What we collect

The demo request form asks for your name, work email address and company. It also has two optional fields: your role, and a free-text description of how your team reviews assets today. Those five fields are the only personal data this site asks you for.

The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and embeds no trackers. Web fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of that request — that is the only third party this page contacts.

Why we collect it

To reply to you and arrange the conversation you asked for. We do not use it for anything else, we do not add you to a marketing list, and we do not sell, rent or share it with third parties.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to delete it, or until it is clear the conversation is not going anywhere — whichever comes first.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, and we will do so. If you are in the UK or EU, the UK GDPR and GDPR give you those rights explicitly; we will honour the same requests wherever you are.
